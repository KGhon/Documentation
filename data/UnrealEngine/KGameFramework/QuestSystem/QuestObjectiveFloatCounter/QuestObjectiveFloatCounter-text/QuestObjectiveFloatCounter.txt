[Title]
UQuestObjectiveFloatCounter

[Definitions]
<b>Type:</b> Class
<b>Inherits:</b> UObject
<b>Category:</b> Unreal Engine > KGameFramework > QuestSystem

[Overview]
A premade QuestObjective that counts to a threshhold to complete.

NOTE: This premade QuestObjective automatically saves all data needed for the class to function. If you require saving additional information there are two options:<ul>
<li>Store the data on the SharedQuestDataObject and handle saving within that class.</li>
<li>Follow the save system guide for saving in derived classes that already implement save and load functionality (QuestObjectives are their own SaveAgent).</li></ul>
