[Title]
UAudioEvent

[Definitions]
<b>Type:</b> Class
<b>Inherits:</b> UDataAsset
<b>Category:</b> KAudioSystem

[Overview]
An AudioEvent allows designers to create premade lists of AudioEventCommands that do multiple things within the KAudioSystem by triggering a single AudioEvent. AudioEvents can be executed using the ExecuteAudioEvent() function from KAudioSystemStatics.
