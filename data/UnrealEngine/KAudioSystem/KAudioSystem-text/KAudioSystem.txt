[Title]
K Audio System

[Definitions]
<b>Type:</b> Unreal Engine Plugin
<b>Category:</b> KAudioSystem

[Overview]
The KAudioSystem's main focus is providing a streamlined development workflow for implementing interactive audio behaviors. The system is written to allow audio designers to author gamaplay responsive audio with a combination of Blueprints and MetaSound while keeping audio implementation separate from other game systems. This system sits alongside Unreal Engine's built-in audio system and does not replace it.
