[Title]
UQuestBlockBase

[Definitions]
<b>Type:</b> Class
<b>Inerits:</b> UObject
<b>Category:</b> KGameFramework > QuestSystem

[Overview]
QuestBlocks are used within QuestGraphs to encapsulate a stage of a quest. Upon completion, QuestBlocks will call an "on complete" event meant to trigger the activation of the next QuestBlock(s) in a QuestGraph. Internally, QuestBlocks are collections of QuestObjectives and evaluate their completion state based on the results of the objectives they contain.

NOTE: QuestBlocks automatically save all data needed for the base class to function. If your QuestBlock requires saving additional information there are two options:<ul>
<li>Store the data on the SharedQuestDataObject and handle saving within that class.</li>
<li>Follow the save system guide for saving in derived classes that already implement save and load functionality (QuestBlocks are their own SaveAgent).</li></ul>
